## Important Findings

### 1. Package Duration Mismatch

**Canadian charter tour operators (Sunwing, SellOff Vacations, Air Canada Vacations, Transat) almost exclusively offer 7-night packages.** No 4-night or 5-night all-inclusive packages were found on any site during this scan. This is the standard model for charter vacation operators in Canada.

### 2. Departure Date Availability

Charter flights from YYZ to Caribbean destinations operate on **fixed weekly schedules** (typically 1-2x per week per destination). The closest departures to your April 23-25 window are:

- **April 22** (Wed) - La Romana, DR
- **April 24** (Fri) - Puerto Plata, DR (Transat/Air Transat)
- **April 26** (Sun) - Cancun, MX (Transat/Air Transat)
- **April 28** (Tue) - Punta Cana, Montego Bay, Negril, Puerto Plata
- **April 29** (Wed) - Cancun, Riviera Maya

### 3. Sunwing BOGO Promotion - DOES NOT APPLY

The "Buy 1 seat, get 2nd at 50% off" promotion is **air-only** (not vacation packages) and the **travel period is May 1 - October 31, 2026**. Your April travel dates fall outside the eligible window.

- Booking window: April 13-30, 2026
- Travel window: May 1 - October 31, 2026
- Source: [Sunwing BOGO page](https://www.sunwing.ca/en/promotion/flights/bogo-flight-deals)

### 4. Air Canada Vacations "50% Off 2nd Traveller" - PARTIALLY APPLIES

Travel from **April 7 - October 31, 2026** qualifies. However, it requires a **minimum 7-night stay** and applies to **select packages only** with non-stop Economy flights. The 50% off the 2nd traveller's flight is distributed as 25% savings per person.

- Booking deadline: May 3, 2026
- Source: [Open Jaw article](https://openjaw.com/newsroom/operators/2026/04/08/save-on-summer-travel-with-air-canada-vacations/)

### 5. Air Canada Vacations "$250/Person" Savings

Save $250 per person on select vacation packages to Mexico, Caribbean, and Central America for departures through April 30, 2026. **This applies** to your dates.

### 6. Transat "It's Worth It" Sale - APPLIES

Packages from **$949/pp** for select departures. Booking deadline: **April 19, 2026** (5 days away). Travel through December 16, 2026. Includes Air Transat flights, resort, transfers, checked bag, carry-on.

- Source: [Boarding Pass Travel](https://www.boardingpasstravel.com/post/transat-all-inclusive-vacation-deals-summer-2026)

## Top 3 Picks

### 1. Best Overall Value: Viva Heavens by Wyndham (Puerto Plata, DR) via Transat

- **Price:** $1,399/pp = **$2,798 total** (save 10%)
- **Departs:** April 24, 7 nights (returns May 1)
- **Why:** This is the only package found that departs in your exact target window (April 24). It's well under your $3,000 budget. Puerto Plata has beautiful north-coast beaches, less crowded than Punta Cana. The Viva brand is family-friendly with buffet dining, pools, and beach access. Air Transat flights included.
- **Caveat:** Puerto Plata wasn't in your priority list, but it's still Dominican Republic with similar vibes. You'd need the full week off (return May 1). The resort is a 3.5-star property.
- **Book by April 19** to get the Transat "It's Worth It" sale pricing.
- [SellOff/Transat listing](https://www.selloffvacations.com/en/vacation-deals/transat)

### 2. Best for Baby Emma: Bluebay Grand Esmeralda (Riviera Maya, Mexico) via Sunwing

- **Price:** $1,635/pp = **$3,270 total** (save 16%)
- **Departs:** April 29, 7 nights
- **Why:** Large family-friendly resort on a calm stretch of Riviera Maya beach. Multiple buffet restaurants (easiest dining with a baby). Known for spacious rooms with balconies, which is ideal for putting Emma down and relaxing on the balcony in the evening. Cribs available on request. The beach is wide and sheltered with calmer water than Cancun's hotel zone.
- **Caveat:** Departs April 29, which is after your target window.
- [Sunwing listing](https://www.sunwing.ca/en/promotion/packages/all-inclusive-vacation-packages)

### 3. Most Romantic (Anniversary Vibes): HM Alma de Bayahibe (La Romana, DR) via Sunwing

- **Price:** $1,685/pp = **$3,370 total** (save 31%)
- **Departs:** April 22, 7 nights (returns April 29)
- **Why:** Bayahibe is one of the most beautiful and tranquil beach areas in the DR -- far quieter than Punta Cana. Smaller boutique-style resort with intimate dining options. Known for gorgeous sunsets, calm waters, and a romantic atmosphere. Balcony rooms often available. **You'd be there for your April 27 anniversary.** The calm, shallow waters are also great for Emma.
- **Caveat:** Depart April 22 (one day early). 7 nights total means extra days off work.
- [Sunwing listing](https://www.sunwing.ca/en/promotion/packages/all-inclusive-vacation-packages)
