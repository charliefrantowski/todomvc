## Important Findings

### 1. Package Duration Mismatch

**Canadian charter tour operators (Sunwing, SellOff Vacations, Air Canada Vacations) almost exclusively offer 7-night packages.** No 4-night or 5-night all-inclusive packages were found on any of the three sites during this scan. This is the standard model for charter vacation operators in Canada.

### 2. Departure Date Availability

Charter flights from YYZ to Caribbean destinations operate on **fixed weekly schedules** (typically 1-2x per week per destination). The exact April 23-25 departure window does not align with most available departures found. The closest departures are:

- **April 22** (Wed) - La Romana, DR
- **April 28** (Tue) - Punta Cana, Montego Bay, Negril, Puerto Plata
- **April 29** (Wed) - Cancun, Riviera Maya

### 3. Sunwing BOGO Promotion - DOES NOT APPLY

The "Buy 1 seat, get 2nd at 50% off" promotion is **air-only** (not vacation packages) and the **travel period is May 1 - October 31, 2026**. Your April 23-29 travel dates fall outside the eligible window.

- Booking window: April 13-30, 2026
- Travel window: May 1 - October 31, 2026
- Source: [Sunwing BOGO page](https://www.sunwing.ca/en/promotion/flights/bogo-flight-deals)

### 4. Air Canada Vacations "50% Off 2nd Traveller" - PARTIALLY APPLIES

This promotion covers travel from **April 7 - October 31, 2026**, so your dates qualify. However, it requires a **minimum 7-night stay** and applies to **select packages only** with non-stop Economy flights. Savings of 50% off the 2nd traveller's flight portion is distributed as 25% per person.

- Booking deadline: May 3, 2026
- Source: [Open Jaw article](https://openjaw.com/newsroom/operators/2026/04/08/save-on-summer-travel-with-air-canada-vacations/)

### 5. Air Canada Vacations "$250/Person" Savings

Save $250 per person on select vacation packages to Mexico, Caribbean, and Central America for departures through April 30, 2026. This **does apply** to your dates.

- Source: [Air Canada Vacations](https://vacations.aircanada.com/en/all-booking-options/all-inclusive)

- **Price:** $2,125/pp = **$4,250 total** (was $2,505/pp, save 15%)

- **Why:** Akumal is known for its calm, shallow, turquoise bay -- one of the best beaches in the Riviera Maya for small children. The resort is smaller and quieter than mega-resorts. Buffet dining available. Cribs typically available on request. The sheltered bay means gentle waves perfect for an 8-month-old's first beach experience.
- **Caveat:** Over budget at $4,250. April 29 departure is late.
- [Sunwing listing](https://www.sunwing.ca/en/promotion/packages/all-inclusive-vacation-packages)

- **Price:** $1,685/pp = **$3,370 total** (was $2,455/pp, save 31%)

Known for gorgeous sunsets, calm waters, and a romantic atmosphere. Balcony rooms often available. The April 22 departure is closest to your target window (you'd be there for your April 27 anniversary).
- **Caveat:** Returns April 29, making it 7 nights (you'd miss a couple extra work days). Over budget at $3,370.

## Recommendations & Next Steps

### The Core Problem: 4-5 Night Packages Don't Exist on These Sites

Canadian charter operators (Sunwing, SellOff, Air Canada Vacations) package vacations as **7-night stays** with charter flights on fixed schedules. This creates two mismatches with your plan:

1. **Duration:** You want 4-5 nights; they only offer 7.
2. **Dates:** Charter flights don't depart daily. No April 23-25 departures were found for your target destinations.

### Option A: Accept a 7-Night Package (Best Value)

If you can take the full week off (depart ~Apr 22, return ~Apr 29), you unlock the best pricing. The **HM Alma de Bayahibe** (Apr 22 dep, $3,370 total) or **Cofresi Palm Beach** (Apr 28 dep, $3,090 total) are closest to budget.

### Option B: Book Flight + Hotel Separately (True 4-5 Nights)

For a genuine 4-night trip (Apr 23-27 or Apr 24-28), book separately:
- **Flights:** Check Air Canada, WestJet, Flair on Google Flights for YYZ to PUJ/CUN/MBJ. Expect $400-700/pp roundtrip.
- **Hotel:** Book 4 nights at an all-inclusive via Expedia.ca, Hotels.com, or direct. Expect $150-300/night for a good all-inclusive.
- **Estimated total:** $2,000-3,500 depending on resort quality.

### Option C: Use Air Canada Vacations with the 50% Off 2nd Traveller Promo

If you go with a 7-night package through Air Canada Vacations, the "50% off 2nd traveller" promo could save $200-500+ on the flight portion. Book by May 3, 2026.
